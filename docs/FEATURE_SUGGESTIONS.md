# Part A: Two features Coy should have

## 1. A first-message nudge after a match

**What:** When two people match, offer three tappable, low-pressure openers (for example "Okay, this is a little awkward") that start a conversation, instead of ending at "It's a match".

**Why it is needed:** The match is the moment of highest emotion in Coy, and also the moment of highest awkwardness. Both people spent weeks wondering and now neither wants to speak first. Without a next step, the match goes quiet and the product's promise ("find out if your crush likes you back") never becomes an actual conversation. Starters remove the "who goes first" problem and make the payoff measurable (match to first-message rate).

## 2. A "hide me" list and add-rate limits

**What:** Let users list phone numbers or emails that must never be matchable with them (exes, coworkers, family), and limit how many crushes any account can add per day.

**Why it is needed:** Coy's core promise is that one-sided interest stays secret. Two things threaten it: people who do not want to be found by certain contacts, and people who add many numbers to probe who is on the app. A hide list gives users control, and rate limits make mass probing expensive. Both strengthen trust in the privacy promise, which is the reason people use Coy at all.
